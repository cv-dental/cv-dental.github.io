Meet the team Get in touch

The problem

Jaw reconstruction is planned slowly, by hand

When a tumour is removed from the jaw, surgeons rebuild it with bone from elsewhere in the body. Planning that rebuild in 3D is skilled, manual and time-consuming, and patients are often already waiting too long.

  • 100–150

    jaw tumour surgeries a year in a single surgical unit

  • ~60%

    of patients are found at an advanced stage

  • ~1month

    waiting for a CT scan

  • up to 1day

    to plan one jaw rebuild by hand

Source: interview with Prof. A.M. Attygalla (2026). Figures are approximate.

How it works

From scan to surgical plan

  1. 01

    CT scan

    A routine CT or CBCT scan of the patient's jaw is the starting point.

  2. 02

    Segment

    AI outlines the bone, teeth and tumour in 3D, automatically.

  3. 03

    Surgeon review

    The surgeon checks and refines the 3D model. The expert stays in control.

  4. 04

    Cutting guide Coming next

    A patient-specific guide to help make the planned cuts precisely in theatre.
